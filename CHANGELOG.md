The renewal cycle, the price re-pricing notices and the operator's decision on a transfer or a card payment (new: payment confirmed, proof rejected) now go through it, in the app and by email, and a notice from the test copy shows marked as such (FR-NOT-1, FR-NOT-2).
- Email templates in the database (migration 0060, ADR-037): the super admin edits each account and notice email's subject, heading, message, button and footer per language in `/admin/emails`, with a preview, a test sent to their own address, publishing and a history. Versions are never edited or reused; the copy in code is the fallback and the first version. Every email still leaves through the worker's one provider, and lint forbids building a provider elsewhere (FR-NOT-3, FR-ADM-4).
- Document and batch notices (migration 0061, ADR-036): the company is told, in the app and by email, when SRI does not authorize a document, when one never reached SRI on its issue day, when SRI has held one for 24 hours, and when a batch of invoices is done; a re-issue shows in the app, and authorizations arrive as one notice with a count per minute, off until a member turns it on. The events are queued in the transaction that changes the document and told by a job to the real company, also for its test copy (FR-NOT-2, FR-DOC-5, FR-BULK-3).
- Certificate and quota warnings (migration 0062, ADR-036): the owners and admins are told, in the app and by email, when the signing certificate ends within 30, 15 and 7 days and when it has ended (a daily 07:00 Quito job), and the billing managers when a period's documents reach 80 % and 100 % of the quota (every 15 minutes). A key per certificate and step, or per quota window and step, tells each once, and only the closest step is told after a gap (FR-NOT-2, FR-ONB-3, FR-BILL-7).
- Plan-change notices (migration 0063, ADR-036): a price change that owes notice is told to the billing managers of every paying company it reaches, and they can't turn it off; a module losing functions or leaving a plan, and a plan limit going down, are told with their date to the companies that hold them. They are sent in the transaction that publishes or edits (FR-BILL-6, ADR-011). The email job now keeps `number`, `count` and `name`, which its schema had dropped: the document, batch, certificate and quota emails went out with those blank (FR-NOT-2).
- Operator notices (migration 0064, ADR-036): every super admin is told, in a new bell in the platform area, a page of their own (Avisos) and by email, when a company reports a transfer and when a card payment is applied. They have their own tables, written only in system context, and a receipt per super admin, so each marks their own as read. A transfer is queued as `platform.notify` in the company's transaction (FR-NOT-2, FR-ADM-2).
- The dashboard (FR-REP-1): the Panel now shows net sales today and this month (authorized invoices less credit notes), documents by status with what is in progress and what has problems highlighted, the top five products of the month, stocked products with nothing left available, the quota used, and the certificate's expiry. Each part shows only what the person's role and plan reach. It is `dashboard()` in the new `reports` module, composed from reads that `documents` and `inventory` expose.
- Reports (FR-REP-2): a Reportes page with sales (by day, client or product, net of credit notes), documents issued, the IVA summary by rate for the monthly declaration, and the receivables aging, each on screen with a date range and as a CSV that opens in Excel (`;` and decimal commas in Spanish). Reading needs `reports.read`, the file `reports.export` (accountants and above). They are one shape, `runReport()`, so one page and one download serve all.
- The rest of the reports and the accountant's export (FR-REP-2, FR-REP-3): stock movements over a range, with Ecuador's day, and purchases by provider (sent orders, not drafts or cancelled ones), on the same page and CSV; and a ZIP on the Reportes page with every authorization the SRI gave in a month, voided documents in their own folder. The ZIP is written with Node's own deflate, no new dependency.
- CSV import and export of products and clients (FR-MD-1, FR-MD-2): Importar CSV and Exportar CSV on both screens. A file is checked whole before anything is written, so one wrong line changes nothing and the dialog names each line and column to fix; a code (products) or identification (clients) already on file is updated and a new one created. The export is the same file in the person's language, with Spanish Excel's `;` and decimal comma, and imports back as no change. The opening-balance import moved onto the same reader and upload dialog (`kernel/csv-table.ts`, `components/csv-import-dialog.tsx`).
- Bulk invoicing from a CSV (FR-BULK-1, ADR-026): Facturación masiva has an Importar CSV page for a file with one line per product, where lines sharing a reference make one invoice (so an invoice can have many products) and the client is a known identification, blank for consumidor final. The whole file is checked before anything is queued: one wrong row starts nothing and every problem comes back by line and column. It needs Bulk invoicing and the catalogue, not the Sales module, so a Bulk-only plan is no longer locked. Each invoice is made by the worker through the single-invoice pipeline, paid in one payment for its total; the stack view shows the file's reference.
- The jobs view (migration 0065, FR-ADM-5): a Trabajos page in the platform area with each queue's counts, schedule, last run and last failure; the failed jobs, by their error and never their data, each with a Reintentar that puts it back in its queue and is recorded in the platform log; and the documents SRI has held for more than 24 hours across companies, with the test copy's marked.
- The platform activity log and invoice-free payments (migration 0066, FR-ADM-1, FR-BILL-4): a Registro page lists what the super admins changed (plans, modules, prices, the IVA, email texts, retried jobs) newest first, with who did it, filterable by kind and paged; the operator can close a verified payment that needs no invoice (its own company, a test) with a reason, which leaves Por facturar, stays on the payment and appears in the company's activity log. Recording an invoice for a payment already closed now says so before looking the invoice up.
- SRI exchanges for support, and their retention (migration 0067, ADR-038, FR-DOC-13): a Respuestas del SRI page finds a document by its access key, in any company, and lists every call made to SRI with its outcome, messages and, while it exists, the raw answer; each lookup is recorded in the platform log. A nightly job clears the raw copy after 90 days, keeping the summary of each call: the app role may update only that column, only to empty it. The periods are in one `RETENTION` object and `docs/legal/retention.md`.
- Milestone 9 gate (`tests/integration/milestone-9-gate.test.ts`): a month of known documents, credit note, voided and unfinished ones, stock movements, purchases and receivables, with the sales (by day, client and product), tax, documents, movements, purchases and aging reports and the dashboard worked out by hand and held to each other; the accountant's ZIP checked against the database; and an email template edited, previewed, published and written into the next email, then put back as a new version (PRD section 15).
- The QA script for the full review (`docs/guides/qa-script.md`): small flows from one local reset, each leaving data for the next, with the legal texts on; `pnpm run-job <queue>` runs a scheduled job now (local and staging only), and `pnpm test-certificate <ruc> <days>` makes a signature that expires that soon.
- A guide to how the repository is organized (`docs/guides/repo-structure.md`): the folders, the layers and which may import which, what each holds and why, where a new thing goes, and one request followed through every layer.
- The repository stands alone: the old-ADR table, PRD Appendix A's porting map and every citation of an old ADR or path into the previous repositories are gone, their reasoning kept in place. When the PRD is silent, the rule is now to decide and record it in an ADR. What is still to copy from them before they are deleted (deploy patterns, the API docs site) is in `NEXT_STEPS.md`.
- Nueva empresa (FR-USR-5): the organization switcher ends with an entry to set up another company, and **Mi cuenta** keeps its button once you have one, so an account can own several companies, each its own RUC, signature and plan. After setting one up, the shell refreshes to it. The RUC in the switcher's menu now has enough contrast.

### Changed

- A required field left empty (or a value outside its range) is now said under the field, in red and in the app's language, with the cursor taken to the first one, instead of the browser's tooltip in the browser's language. It applies to every field built with the shared form field.
- The operator area is called Administración (Administration), not Plataforma (Platform), in menus, titles and messages.
- Empresa turns off **Agregar establecimiento** and **Agregar punto** at the plan's limit and says why, with a link to the plan, as Usuarios does for invitations; core still refuses past the limit (FR-PLAN-3).
- Accepting the legal texts takes one tick per document still pending, instead of one for all three; each is still recorded as its own acceptance (FR-ONB-8).
- The IVA on subscriptions moves from `IVA_RATE` to a dated table with its history (migration 0057, ADR-034), seeded with 15 % from 1 April 2024 and edited on the platform's Precios page. On the day a new rate starts, the daily job re-prices unpaid open payments and emails the payer the new total; a reported transfer keeps its rate. `IVA_RATE` is no longer read (FR-BILL-2).
- Going live can copy the test copy's active products, clients, providers and invoice templates to the real company, once and in the go-live transaction: what the real company already has is left alone, and templates are moved to the copies. The go-live event records how many (FR-ONB-5, ADR-025).
- An invoice stops offering "Nota de crédito" once nothing is left to credit (FR-SAL-8).
- The nightly stock check: the `inventory.verify` job compares every level with the sums of its ledger and logs any drift (FR-INV-2, NFR-JOB-2, ADR-018).

- The invoice form says which line and field is missing or refused ("Falta el código de la línea 1", or "Línea 1: código" for a refusal from core), moves to the field, and keeps the error on the form after the confirmation box is closed.

- Actividad shows an operator who acted on a company (verifying a payment) as "Comprobify" instead of their name, unless they belong to that company.

- Actividad, viewed from the test copy, now includes the changes made to the company itself (accepting the legal texts, tax data, plan), which are recorded on the real company and were missing there (FR-NOT-4).

- An email verification link can no longer be used twice: once the account is verified it is refused, instead of verifying and signing the person in again (FR-ONB-1).
- The confirm dialog wraps a long button label, so "Cerrar sesión en todos los dispositivos" no longer scrolls sideways.
- A renewal paid before its period starts no longer applies what was due at the end straight away: the company keeps the plan it paid for until then, and the daily job moves it on that day (ADR-035).
- A paid plan started while the operator's removal of an add-on is still pending no longer charges that add-on, and the add-on goes as soon as the first payment is verified, instead of staying free until its old date (ADR-030).
- The RIDE draws a line under the last payment row (FR-DOC-10, ADR-015).
- A RIMPE negocio popular company can issue invoices: its legend, `CONTRIBUYENTE NEGOCIO POPULAR - RÉGIMEN RIMPE`, is added to the bundled factura XSD's pattern, which SRI publishes with the emprendedor legend only (ADR-013).
- Milestone 3's gate passed: an invoice authorized by SRI's test environment with a real certificate.
- The SRI schemas live in the repo as the files SRI publishes (`packages/core/assets/xsd/`), with a test that holds the embedded copies to them; the nota de crédito XSD is there for Milestone 4 (ADR-013).
- Credit notes can be created and corrected: from an authorized invoice of an identified buyer, each line held to what its invoice line has left in quantity and amount, with the invoice locked so two at once can't overspend it. A credit note SRI returned releases its share until corrected. Enabled per issue point with the Notas de crédito module (FR-SAL-8, FR-DOC-9, ADR-016).
- Credit note screens and RIDE: an invoice that SRI authorized offers "Nota de crédito", which opens a form listing each line with what is left to credit (a line can't go over it, said before sending); the credit note's page shows "Modifies" and the invoice lists its notes, with their status. A returned credit note is corrected on the same form. `issuingOptions` takes the document type; `allowed.credit` and `creditNoteDraft` feed the screens. The RIDE reads a nota de crédito from its authorized XML (the document it modifies and the reason, no payments), and the buyer's email job now covers it (FR-DOC-9, FR-DOC-10, FR-DOC-12).
- Guía de remisión builder: `buildGuiaRemision` writes XSD 1.1.0's elements in order (departure address, carrier and plate, transport dates, recipients each with an optional supporting document and their goods, with no prices or taxes), and `guiaDateProblem` holds the dates to SRI's rule (start not before the issue date, error 82; end not before the start). It signs and validates like the other types (FR-SAL-9).
- Guías de remisión can be created and corrected, from an invoice (its buyer, goods and supporting document fill the form) or on their own: carrier, plate, transport dates, departure address and recipients with their goods. Dates are held to SRI's rule before signing, a guía is corrected rather than re-issued, it consumes quota and needs the Guías de remisión module (now available). The detail page shows the transport and links both ways with its invoice, and the RIDE draws the guía from its authorized XML. A guía is dated by its transport start, which is what SRI checks its access key against. The form searches clients, products and authorized invoices (picking an invoice fills the recipient) (ADR-017, FR-SAL-9, FR-DOC-10, FR-DOC-12).
- The nota de crédito builder: the XML of schema 1.1.0 with the original's number and date and the reason, rounded like the factura, signed and re-dated like it. The factura's header and tax blocks moved to a shared module (FR-SAL-8, ADR-013).
- A credit note line is a return (quantity at a price) or an amount (a discount or adjustment, written as quantity 1): an amount holds the invoice line's value but not its quantity, so a price credit no longer uses up the units that can still come back (migration 0036, FR-SAL-8, ADR-016).
- A document can come from another: a credit note from its invoice, a guía optionally from one. `documents.related_document_id` has a composite foreign key (so it can't cross companies), is required for a credit note and refused for a factura, and never changes. The document detail returns the origin and what came from it (FR-SAL-8, FR-SAL-9).
- A registry of the document types SRI's engine issues (factura, nota de crédito, guía de remisión): XSD validation, the re-issue check and the buyer's email read it. The nota de crédito and guía schemas are embedded and validate; the RIMPE legend widening covers all three (FR-DOC-1, ADR-013).

- Onboarding asks for the tax regime, so a RIMPE company's first invoice carries its legend; it stays editable in Configuración (FR-ONB-2).
